**ProductiveX AI** won **Best Use of Composio MCP** ($4k in credits) at **Agents in the Loop 2025** in San Francisco on August 2.

It is a voice-activated productivity agent. Email, calendar, and tasks come in as a mess. ProductiveX turns them into one daily brief: what is urgent, what is on the calendar, and what to do next.

## How It Works

- **Vapi** handles the voice conversation
- **Composio MCP** connects the real productivity tools
- **Claude on AWS Bedrock** does the reasoning
- **Temporal** keeps flaky email and calendar APIs from breaking the workflow, with saga-style retries

The point is not another inbox. It is one layer that already knows the day.
