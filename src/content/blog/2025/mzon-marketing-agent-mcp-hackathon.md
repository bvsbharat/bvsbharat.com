Hack win **#30**. At the **MCP AI Agents Hackathon** by Creators Corner, **Mzon** took three sponsored awards from one event:

- **Top Airia Agent**
- **Best Use of Bright Data MCP** for web access in AI agents
- **Best Use of TigerData**

Mzon is an AI content and news intelligence platform. It streams real-time news so campaigns stay current, generates channel-specific posts with live previews, and manages campaigns, assets, and voice-to-content workflows. Bright Data supplies the news, TigerData stores it, and Airia runs the agent.

I built the whole thing from my phone.

## What It Does

- **Live news** keeps campaigns tied to what is happening now
- **Channel-specific content** with previews and mockups for Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and more
- **Campaign and asset management**, including voice-to-content

## Built With

Airia, Bright Data, TigerData, Stytch, HoneyHive, React, and FastAPI.
