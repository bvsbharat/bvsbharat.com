Four months away from hackathons. Came back and won.

This is hack win **#40** and first place at **The Executable World**, with support from **Tencent EdgeOne Makers**. I built **SimVal.Ai**, a self-improvement layer for voice and chat agents.

## What SimVal.Ai Does

Reliability comes from the loop, not from a single prompt rewrite:

- **Simulates caller personas** — including distressed callers and callers missing information
- **Scores every run independently**
- **Rewrites the prompts** and retests only the failures
- **Promotes or rolls back** each version based on those results

Agents do not get reliable from a better prompt alone. They get reliable when something tests them, measures the run, and fixes the failures on its own.
