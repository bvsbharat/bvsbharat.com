Hack win **#35**, and first place with the team at the **MeetStream.ai x Scalekit** voice-agent hackathon in San Francisco.

## What We Built

A real-time medical voice agent that joins live patient intake calls and acts as a co-pilot for healthcare staff. The provider stays with the patient. The agent works alongside the conversation.

- **Listens to the intake call** as it happens
- **Drafts and sends follow-up emails**
- **Updates patient records** on the fly
- **Surfaces relevant medical history**, including critical context such as allergies, past diagnoses, and open referrals

**Scalekit** scopes every data access to the right patient and provider, so records do not bleed across accounts. **MeetStream.ai** supplied the live audio, transcription, and bot lifecycle events the agent acts on.

The demo was the workflow itself: spoken words become structured action while the conversation is still going, instead of forms filled in after the call.
