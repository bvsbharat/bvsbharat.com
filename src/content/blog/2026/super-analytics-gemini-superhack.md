Finalist at the **Gemini 3 SuperHack** in San Francisco on January 31, 2026, hosted by Cerebral Valley with Google DeepMind, and winner of the **Stream Vision Agents** prize ($5k in Stream credits).

I built **Super Analytics**, a real-time sports analytics platform that works like a computational sideline assistant. It takes live game footage and turns it into positional analytics, strategic insights, and visual decision matrices for coaches, teams, and analysts during the match. Coaches need strategy answers at halftime, not after the game.

The build uses Google Gemini, Stream WebRTC, and Vision Agents.
