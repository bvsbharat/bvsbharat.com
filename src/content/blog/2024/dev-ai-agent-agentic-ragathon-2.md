**Dev AI Agent** placed **2nd** in the Continuous Innovation Track at LlamaIndex's **Agentic RAG-A-THON 2**, October 11–13, 2024.

Dev AI Agent is a coding assistant that runs on your machine. It builds the app — components, pages, and APIs — and can automate most of the development work, across stacks such as Next.js and Angular. The local version keeps the code on device, including Llama 3.1 on an M1.

This is a separate result from the Best Use of Llama 3.1 award at the AI Agent Hackathon earlier that fall. Same project, different event: runner-up on the Continuous Innovation Track at the LlamaIndex RAG-A-THON.

## Built With

LlamaIndex, Llama 3.1, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Pinecone.
