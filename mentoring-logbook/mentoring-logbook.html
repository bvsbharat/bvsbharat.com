HOW TO USE THIS LOGBOOK

This book is for one faculty mentor and three Phase I mentees (NMC CBME 2024 ratio 1:3). Meet about once a month. There are 12 meeting sheets per mentee for this academic year. Add extra sheets later if needed.

Before the first meeting

  1. Confirm the cover (already printed with your name) and add academic year / batch.
  2. Complete one Student profile with each mentee in the Foundation Course week.
  3. Fill SWOC together. Keep health and family issues brief; use the confidential note if needed.

Each monthly meeting (aim: 15–20 minutes)

  1. Open that mentee’s next numbered sheet. Write date, time and place first.
  2. Tick topics. Write only three short notes: issue → student action → your follow-up.
  3. Both sign. Write the next date before they leave.
  4. Tick the yearly tracker (front) so you can see missed months at a glance.

What to cover over the year (not all in one meeting)

Adjustment & hostel · attendance (75% eligibility) · Anatomy / Physiology / Biochemistry · internal assessment · FAP · AETCOM · exam stress · sleep/health · ragging/safety · career curiosity · parent contact as required by college.

Escalate the same day (do not only write it here): thoughts of self-harm, ragging, sexual harassment, acute medical illness, or a student who stops attending. Inform the Mentoring cell / MEU / Dean as per college SOP. Record “referred on (date)” — not the clinical detail.
Privacy. This is a faculty log. Do not leave it in a shared staff room unlocked. Do not photograph pages onto a class WhatsApp group. Parent meeting records may be required by UGMEB/NMC — keep those factual and short.
