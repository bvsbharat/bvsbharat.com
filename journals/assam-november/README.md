# Assam Field Journal · November

A 6-page A5 travel journal for a Vizag → Bhubaneswar → Guwahati journey, three nights at The Postcard in the Durrung Tea Estate, and Kaziranga booked through the estate.

## Print

1. Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the PDF.
2. Paper: **A5** is the designed size (148 × 210 mm). On A4, print at 100% or “fit to printable area” and trim if you like.
3. Colour, single-sided or double-sided. If double-sided, flip on the **long edge**.
4. Slightly off-white or cream paper is kinder than bright white.
5. Take a **pencil**. Leave the last page until the flight home.

## Pages

1. Cover — name, dates, route
2. The going — flights, Guwahati pickup, packing
3. Durrung — daily log, tea tasting, table
4. Kaziranga — safari log, wildlife ticks, sketch
5. Slower notes — sounds, Assamese, a pocket for a leaf
6. Homeward — return flights, three things to keep
